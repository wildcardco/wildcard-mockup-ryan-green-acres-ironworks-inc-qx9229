The Shop

An ironworks, not a fence catalog.

Green Acres Ironworks, Inc. is a hands-on fabrication shop on Kennedy Avenue in Schererville. We specialize in designing and fabricating unique, handcrafted railings, stairs, gates and fences.

Homeowners come to us for metalwork that's durable and theirs alone. Remodelers and builders come to us for a fabrication partner who delivers custom iron components that fit the first time.

  • Hand-forged detailing — ornament shaped in the shop.
  • One-off fabrication — built to your measurements, not pulled off a shelf.
  • Structural metalwork — integrated into decks, porches, and stairs.
  • Residential & light commercial — homes, properties, and contractor jobs.
