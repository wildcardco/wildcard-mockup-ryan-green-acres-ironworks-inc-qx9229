The Shop

Welders first. Craftsmen always.

We are a working metal shop on Kennedy Avenue in Schererville. When off-the-shelf railing won't fit the stair, the house, or the way you want it to look, that's where we come in.

We draw it, cut it, weld it and finish it ourselves. Straight lines or scrollwork, a single window guard or a full balcony, the work gets the same attention.

  • Handcrafted, made to orderBuilt for your opening and your design, not pulled from a mass-produced kit.
  • Structural and decorative under one roofStairs and balconies that carry load and still look like a piece of craft.
  • Local with direct shop accessBased in Schererville, serving Northwest Indiana and the Chicagoland South Suburbs.
